💳 How Payment Verification Works

  1. Select the rank or crate key quantity you wish to buy and click Purchase or checkout from your Cart.
  2. Scan the displayed UPI QR Code using GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or any UPI app.
  3. Enter your exact Minecraft Username and the 12-Digit UTR / Transaction ID from your UPI payment receipt.
  4. Click Submit Payment Log. Our staff team verifies the log and grants your items in-game shortly!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

⏱️ How long do purchases take?

Once you submit your 12-digit UTR, payment logs are processed by server staff. Items are usually delivered within 5 to 30 minutes.

🌐 How do I join the server?

Open Minecraft (Java or Bedrock), go to Multiplayer > Add Server, and use the IP: dioxide.pixelforge.gg.

⚠️ Didn't receive your items?

If your items are delayed past 1 hour, open a ticket on our Discord server or send us an email with your UTR screenshot.

💬 Need Direct Support?

Our support team is available on Discord and via email to assist you with store orders, player reports, or technical issues.
